2026-05-01

Settlement Onboarding Flow

We reworked the first-hour onboarding to reduce friction for new players. The helper prompt system now better introduces farming, building priorities, and role assignment. NPC guidance appears in a clearer sequence, so players can follow their preferred playstyle faster.

2026-04-24

Immersive Fishing Balance Pass

Immersive fishing now has a more meaningful risk-reward profile. Reward values were tuned to reflect time investment while fatigue penalties were adjusted to keep late-session decisions strategic instead of punitive.

2026-04-12

School & Hospital UI Prototype

We shipped a revised prototype for school and hospital management screens. The update clarifies progression status, staffing logic, and upgrade affordances, making the long-term development loop easier to understand at a glance.

2026-04-03

Screenshot Pipeline Preparation

Internal capture workflow placeholders were added so future media updates can be published faster. This groundwork keeps gallery updates consistent with milestone builds.
